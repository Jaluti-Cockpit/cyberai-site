How we build

Four stages, each priced as a fixed fee. You can stop after any of them and keep what was built.

  1. 01 Baseline We start from your own data and alarm history, and size the work with today's numbers.
  2. 02 Design Each agent gets a written role, its allowed actions, its limits and a test suite built from your past events.
  3. 03 Run beside you It runs next to your staff at one site. Authority passes over one flow at a time, only as the tests are met.
  4. 04 Hand over Your team or ours runs it across more sites, with the control sheet, tests and record handed over too.
